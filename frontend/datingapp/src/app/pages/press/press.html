What is Rosemarry?

Rosemarry is an early-stage dating app built around small weekly Circles. Compatible people spend six days getting to know one another through group conversation, prompts and shared activities, with the freedom to match at any time.

Product status: In development and not yet available to download. Launch timing and locations have not been announced.
