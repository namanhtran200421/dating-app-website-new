Why Rosemarry exists

The dating app formula needs a rewrite.

The usual formula asks people to swipe faster, spend more, and trust a black-box algorithm to decide who deserves to be seen. That might create activity, but it does not always create connection.

We believe a dating product can be intentional without becoming intense, safer without feeling clinical, and useful without hiding every good feature behind another payment.

So we’re building the app we wanted to use: more thoughtful, more transparent, and a lot less cringe.
