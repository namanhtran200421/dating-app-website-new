Why Rosemarry exists ?

The dating app
formula needs a rewrite

The usual formula asks people to swipe faster, spend more, and trust a black-box algorithm to decide who deserves to be seen.
That might create activity, but it does not always create connection.

We believe a dating app needs to be accessible, safe and useful without hiding every good
feature behind another payment.

So we’re building the app we wanted to use, fairer, cheaper and safer,
and of course smarter :)

Built by people who get it.

The faces, stories, and slightly over-caffeinated brains behind Rosemarry.
