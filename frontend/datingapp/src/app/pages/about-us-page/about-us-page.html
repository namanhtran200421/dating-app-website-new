Our story · written in Melbourne

The dating app formula needs a rewrite.

Rosemarry started with a simple frustration: more profiles were not creating better connections.

So we changed the pace ↗

Dear dating apps,

The usual formula asks people to make quick decisions from a few photos, keep swiping, and trust an algorithm to find the right person.

That creates more choices, but not always better connections.

We believe attraction often needs time, familiarity and real interaction to grow. So Rosemarry is built around small weekly Circles, shared conversations and repeated encounters.

Less swiping. More getting to know each other. — The Rosemarry team
