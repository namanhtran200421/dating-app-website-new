Why Rosemarry exists

The dating app formula needs a rewrite.

The usual formula asks people to swipe faster, spend more, and trust a black-box algorithm to decide who deserves to be seen. That might create activity, but it does not always create connection.

We believe a dating app needs to be accessible, safe and useful without hiding every good feature behind another payment.

So we’re building the app we wanted to use: fairer, cheaper and safer.

Built by people who get it.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

Is Rosemarry actually free to use? +

Yes. Core matching, messaging, and safety features stay free. Paid plans just unlock extras like more likes and boosts.

When is Rosemarry launching? +

We’re in early access right now. Drop your email in the signup box below and we’ll let you know the moment we’re live.

How is Rosemarry different from other dating apps? +

No hidden desirability score, no pay-to-win visibility, and no surprise paywalls — just clearer pricing and safer matching.

Is my data safe with Rosemarry? +

We only use what we need to help you match well, and we never sell your data to third parties.
