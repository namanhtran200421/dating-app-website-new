How Rosemarry works

A dating app built around conversation, not endless swiping

Rosemarry is an early-stage dating app in development. The plan is to introduce you to a small weekly Circle of compatible people nearby, with five days to talk, try shared activities and decide whether you want to match.

See the four steps

The short version

One Circle. Five days. Four simple steps.

This is the planned experience. Rosemarry is not available to download yet, and the details may change as we test the product.

  1. 01

    Tell us what matters

    Share your preferences and enough about yourself for Rosemarry to look for compatible people nearby.

  2. 02

    Join a weekly Circle

    A Circle brings 10 compatible people together for a five-day cycle instead of an endless stream of profiles.

  3. 03

    Talk and do things together

    Group conversation, prompts and shared activities create more context than a quick first impression.

  4. 04

    Match when it feels right

    You can choose to match during the Circle. When the cycle ends, a new Circle can begin.

A different rhythm

Weekly Circles compared with a swipe feed

Dating apps vary, but this table shows the product distinction Rosemarry is designed around.

Part of the experience Rosemarry's plan A typical swipe feed
Discovery A limited weekly group A continuing queue of profiles
First interaction Talk and join activities before matching Usually match before messaging
Pace A five-day Circle cycle A self-directed stream
Context Repeated group interactions Profile photos and prompts

Choice and safety

You stay in control

The product plan includes identity checks, reporting, blocking, moderation and controls over future Circles. Those safeguards are still being developed and will be documented in detail before launch.
