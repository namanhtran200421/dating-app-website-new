How it works

One week.
Four simple steps.

A slower, smaller way to meet—shown in one product walkthrough and four clear moments.

  1. Tell us what matters.

    Share your interests, preferences and location so we can find people with something meaningful in common.

  2. Start small, not endless.

    Rosemarry brings a small group of compatible people together for one week—no split-second swiping required.

  3. Let personality show up.

    Try prompts, activities and group chat. Shared moments make conversation feel more natural.

  4. Match—or meet again.

    Like someone anytime. When the week ends, strong connections may return in a future Circle.
