The Rosemarry approach

Chemistry needs room to grow.

Rosemarry turns dating into a shared, week-long experience – then uses what it learns to make the next circle more compatible.

Your week on Rosemarry

7days Meet Prompt Chat Regroup

A circle that evolves.

Each week gives people time to become familiar. The next one builds on the connections that actually formed.

  1. 01
    Shape your starting point

    Tell us what matters.

    Sign up and answer questions about your interests, preferences and what you are looking for. Those answers help us find people with meaningful things in common.

  2. 02
    Meet your weekly circle

    Start small, not endless.

    Rosemarry automatically brings a small group of compatible people together for one week, so nobody is reduced to a split-second swipe.

  3. 03
    Share more than a bio

    Let personality show up.

    Answer prompts, try activities and talk in the group chat. Shared moments create natural conversation and show how people actually connect.

  4. 04
    Refresh with more context

    Make the next circle smarter.

    When the week ends, the circle expires automatically. Rosemarry’s AI analyses how everyone interacted and may place highly compatible people together again.
