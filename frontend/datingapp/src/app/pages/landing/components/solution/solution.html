The Rosemarry approach

Better dates need a better system.

More meaningful interactions, better conversations, better matches, and fairer pricing.

Weekly circles

Meet more than a profile.

Rosemarry places you in a small group of compatible people nearby, giving you time to talk, interact and see familiar faces again.

Compatibility

More interaction, better fit

Rosemarry learns from shared interests, prompts, activities and how people naturally interact to improve future circles.

Shared activities

Conversation doesn’t have to start with “hey.”

Answer prompts, play small activities and react to other people in your circle. Shared experiences give you something natural to talk about.

Repeated interaction

Attraction has time to grow.

People you genuinely connect with can appear in future circles, giving you more chances to get to know each other before deciding where it goes.
