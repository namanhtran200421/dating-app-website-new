Rosemarry, clearly

The quick answers.

Current, plain-language facts about Rosemarry, last reviewed 5 September 2026.

01

What is Rosemarry?

Rosemarry is an early-stage dating app built around small weekly Circles. It gives compatible people time to talk, try shared activities and get familiar before deciding whether to match.

02

How do weekly Circles work?

A Circle brings a small group of compatible people together for six days of group chat, prompts and activities. Members can match at any time. A new Circle begins after the current one ends.

Read the full Circle explanation
03

Is Rosemarry available yet?

No. As of 5 September 2026, Rosemarry is still in development and is not available to download. Launch timing and locations have not been announced. People can join the early list for product updates.

04

What is free, and what costs money?

The current plan keeps weekly Circles, discovery, matching and messaging free. An optional Advanced plan is intended to add visibility, discovery and control features. Features and prices may change before launch.

05

Who is building it?

Rosemarry is being built by co-founders Steve Tran, Felix Vu and Samuel Nicholas.

06

How are privacy and safety handled?

Planned safeguards include identity checks, reporting, blocking, moderation and controls over future Circles. The public policy explains current commitments and may evolve before launch.
