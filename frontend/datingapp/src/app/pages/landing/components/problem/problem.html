Why dating apps fall short

Dating apps rush attraction.

A few photos and a split-second decision are expected to predict chemistry. But the people we grow to like are not always the ones we would choose at first glance.

Flat first impressions

A profile is not a person.

Photos and a short bio cannot show how someone listens, jokes or makes other people feel. Personality needs room to show up.

Endless options

More profiles do not create better connections.

When there is always someone new one swipe away, it is easy to move on before curiosity has time to become a connection.

No familiarity

Potential matches disappear too quickly.

In real life, attraction often grows through repeated conversations and shared moments. Most apps replace familiar faces before that can happen.

Cold conversations

A match does not give you something to say.

One-to-one chats with a stranger start from nothing. Without shared context, even a promising match can stall at “hey.”
