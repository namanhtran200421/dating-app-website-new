Dating app in a nutshell

Dating apps are shallow

A few photos, prompts and seconds of attention are expected to tell you whether someone is worth knowing. Real attraction often needs more time than that.

Single impression

A profile isn't a person

Photos, prompts and a short bio can only tell you so much. People are often passed on before their personality ever has a chance to show.

Endless swiping

More people doesn't mean better connections.

Constantly seeing someone new makes it easy to move on instead of giving time for attraction to develop.

Strangers stay strangers

You rarely get the chance to see someone twice.

In real life, attraction often grows after seeing, talking to and spending time around someone repeatedly. Most dating apps constantly replace familiar faces with new ones.

The match graveyard

Getting a match is only the beginning.

Matching someone from a profile doesn't automatically create something to talk about. Without shared experiences or familiarity, conversations often start from zero.
