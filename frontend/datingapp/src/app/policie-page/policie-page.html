Privacy policy

Privacy at a glance

02

The details

Information we collect

The information we receive depends on how you interact with Rosemarry. It may include:

What you give us

  • Name, email address, date of birth, and account details.
  • Profile content such as photos, prompts, preferences, and interests.
  • Messages you send to us, including support requests and feedback.
  • Payment and subscription details processed by our payment providers.

What we receive automatically

  • Device, browser, IP address, and approximate location information.
  • How you use the website or service, including pages and features viewed.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used for essential functions and analytics.
  • Safety signals such as reports, blocks, and suspected misuse.
Sensitive information deserves extra care. Dating profiles may reveal things like gender, sexual orientation, beliefs, or relationship preferences. Where required, we ask for your consent before processing this information, and you choose what appears on your profile.
03

Why we need it

How we use information

We use personal information only when we have a valid reason to do so, including to:

  • create and manage accounts, profiles, subscriptions, and waitlist sign-ups;
  • show relevant profiles and personalise the Rosemarry experience;
  • process transactions and send service-related messages;
  • respond to questions, feedback, and support requests;
  • detect spam, fraud, harassment, and other behaviour that may put people at risk;
  • understand performance, fix issues, and improve our products;
  • meet legal obligations and enforce these policies; and
  • send marketing where you have agreed to receive it or where otherwise permitted.

We rely on your consent, our contract with you, legitimate interests, and legal obligations as applicable. You can withdraw consent at any time, but this will not affect processing that already occurred lawfully.

04

Who sees what

Sharing & retention

When information may be shared

We may share information with trusted providers that help us operate Rosemarry, such as hosting, analytics, payment, communication, moderation, and security services. They may only use information for the work they perform for us and must protect it appropriately.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to investigate or prevent harm, to protect our rights or the rights of others, or as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of assets. Profile information you choose to publish may be visible to other Rosemarry users.

How long we keep it

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, including safety, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Where it may go

Our providers may process information in other countries. When required, we use safeguards designed to protect information during international transfers.

05

Protecting the vibe

Safety & security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your login details private.

Dating safely is a shared assignment. Keep early conversations on-platform, never send money or financial details, meet in a public place, tell someone you trust about your plans, and report behaviour that feels unsafe. If there is an immediate threat, contact local emergency services.

Community expectations

Rosemarry is for respectful, genuine connection. Harassment, hate speech, threats, stalking, scams, impersonation, non-consensual sexual content, exploitation, and illegal activity are not allowed. We may review reports and restrict or remove access when necessary to protect the community.

06

You’re in control

Your choices & rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, object to or limit certain processing, and withdraw consent. You may also be able to complain to your local privacy regulator.

  • Update profile and account details through the service where available.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing using the link in any marketing email.
  • Adjust browser controls for non-essential cookies.
  • Ask us to action a privacy request through our contact page.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We will not treat you unfairly for exercising a privacy right.
