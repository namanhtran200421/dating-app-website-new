Circle

Circle is Rosemarry’s group-based dating feature.

Rosemarry may place eligible users into a Circle based on factors including:

  • Age preferences.
  • Distance.
  • Gender and orientation compatibility.
  • Dating intentions.
  • Dealbreakers.
  • Relationship goals.
  • Lifestyle.
  • Interests.
  • Values and preferences.
  • Previous interactions.
  • Likes and Passes.

Users who have Passed, blocked or reported each other will generally not be placed together again.

Users who have already matched will normally be separated in future Circles.

Rosemarry may prioritise placing users together again where previous interactions suggest that further interaction may be worthwhile.

This can include:

  • One-sided Likes.
  • Strong reciprocal interactions.
  • High compatibility.
  • Repeated conversation or activity engagement.

Being placed with another user again is not guaranteed.

Circle Size

A Circle will normally contain approximately 8–12 users, with around 10 users being the target.

The exact number of users may vary depending on the number of suitable users available.

Rosemarry may prioritise compatibility over reaching a specific Circle size.

Circle Themes

A Circle may be organised around a shared interest or theme.

Examples may include gaming, food, music, hiking, movies or other interests.

Users do not necessarily need to share exactly the same interests.

Compatibility between users takes priority over the Circle theme.

Circle Variety

Rosemarry may combine:

  • Users you have not previously met.
  • Users you have previously shared a Circle with.
  • Users with whom you have previously interacted positively.

Rosemarry will generally avoid recreating exactly the same Circle repeatedly.

5

Likes, Passes and Matches in Circle

While participating in a Circle, you may:

  • Like another member.
  • Pass on another member.
  • Take no action.

One-Sided Like

If you Like another Circle member and they have not Liked you:

  • Your Like remains hidden while the Circle is active.
  • A Match is not created.

A one-sided Like may increase the likelihood that the two users are placed together again, but this is not guaranteed.

Mutual Like

If two users Like each other:

  • A Match is created immediately.
  • Both users are informed.
  • Private messaging becomes available.
  • Both users may remain in their current Circle until it ends.

Pass

If either user Passes:

  • The other user is not notified of the Pass.
  • Any existing one-sided Like between those users is closed.
  • Rosemarry will generally avoid placing those users together again.

No Action

Taking no action is treated as neutral.

Users who take no action may still be placed together again in a future Circle.

End of a Circle

At the end of a Circle, unresolved one-sided Likes may be revealed to the user who received them.

The receiving user may then:

  • Like back and create a Match.
  • Pass.
  • Take no action.

Once a one-sided Like has been revealed, it may remain visible even if the users later share another Circle.
